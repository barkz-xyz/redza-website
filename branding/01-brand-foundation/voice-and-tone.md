# Voice and tone

## Voice

Direct, calm, operational and human. Redza sounds like the person who knows what is moving, where it is going and what needs to happen next.

## Do

- Use short sentences.
- Lead with the operational fact.
- Prefer active verbs: coordinamos, cargamos, movemos, entregamos.
- Explain the next step clearly.
- Show real work and real context.
- Write in Chilean Spanish without exaggerated sales language.

## Avoid

- Empty superlatives such as "los mejores" without evidence.
- Overpromising delivery times or coverage.
- Dense paragraphs on social graphics.
- Trend-chasing that makes the brand look unserious.
- Stock photos that do not resemble Redza's operation.
- Fake testimonials or incentivized reviews.

## Examples

Weak: `Somos la mejor empresa de transporte de Chile.`

Redza: `Preparamos la carga, coordinamos la ruta y mantenemos la operacion en movimiento.`

Weak: `Contactanos para saber mas.`

Redza: `Escribenos por WhatsApp y cuentanos que necesitas mover.`

## Caption structure

1. Hook - one concrete operational truth.
2. Proof - what the image shows.
3. Relevance - why it matters to a business.
4. CTA - one action only.
