# Redza brand foundation

## Brand definition

Redza Transporte y Logistica is a dependable B2B transport and logistics partner for companies that need cargo moved with coordination, visibility and operational discipline.

## Position

For retailers, distribution teams, manufacturers and operations managers in Chile, Redza provides practical transport and distribution support with real operational capacity, direct communication and a route-first mindset.

## Promise

Tu carga no se detiene.

## Supporting idea

Movemos lo que importa.

## Brand attributes

- Precisa: information is clear, useful and specific.
- Operativa: show the work, not only the promise.
- Directa: make it easy to ask for a quote.
- Confiable: use real trucks, real routes, real proof and honest claims.
- Moderna: use a clean visual system with strong contrast and disciplined layouts.

## Audience

- Logistics and operations managers.
- Procurement and supply-chain contacts.
- Retail and distribution businesses.
- Manufacturers and industrial suppliers.
- Companies that need recurring or route-based cargo support.

## Customer problems to address

- Unclear transport coordination.
- Delays that affect the rest of the operation.
- Difficulty finding a responsive local logistics partner.
- Lack of visibility before dispatch.
- Need for a flexible provider that can discuss the route directly.

## Content pillars

1. Operacion real - loading, dispatch, vehicles and preparation.
2. Cobertura y rutas - Chile, routes, destinations and journey context.
3. Confianza B2B - proof, testimonials, process and customer outcomes.
4. Conversion - quote prompts, WhatsApp, service explanations and next steps.

## Message ladder

- First impression: `Tu carga no se detiene.`
- Reason to believe: `Coordinamos cada etapa para que tu operacion siga avanzando.`
- Service explanation: `Transporte y distribucion nacional.`
- Action: `Hablemos de tu proxima ruta.`

## Claims discipline

Use only claims that can be supported by Redza's real operation, service area, equipment or customer permission. Never promise a delivery time, coverage area or capacity that has not been confirmed for the specific quote.
