# Customer testimonial template

## Permission first

Ask the customer for written permission to publish their name, logo, quote, route or image.

## Capture format

- Customer/company:
- Industry:
- Service or route:
- Problem before Redza:
- What Redza coordinated:
- Result or operational benefit:
- Approved quote:
- Approved image/logo:
- Permission date:

## Social structure

`[Customer] needed [operational need]. Redza coordinated [specific action] so [approved result].`
