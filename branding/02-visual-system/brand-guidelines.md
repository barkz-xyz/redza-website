# Redza visual guidelines

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0B0B0C` | Primary dark field, text on light surfaces |
| Soft black | `#151517` | Secondary dark field, cards, footer |
| Red | `#E21A16` | Primary action, signal, accent |
| Red dark | `#BB1512` | Small text on light backgrounds |
| Cream | `#F3F3F1` | Warm light field, paper, section backgrounds |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Logo, reversed text, clean surfaces |

## Typography

- Manrope 800 for headlines.
- Manrope 600-700 for subheads, labels and CTA text.
- Manrope 400-500 for body copy.
- DM Mono 400-500 for operational labels, route codes, numbers and metadata.

## Layout language

- Use generous margins and a strong grid.
- Prefer one large idea per graphic.
- Use red as a signal, not as a full-page default.
- Pair a real operational photo with a dark or cream information field.
- Use thin rules, route lines, coordinates and numbered labels sparingly.
- Keep the logo clear and never place it over a busy photographic area without a controlled field.

## Logo usage

- Use the real white SVG variants from `public/images/redza branding/white svgs/` on dark fields.
- Use the real black SVG variants from `public/images/redza branding/black svgs/` on light fields.
- Use the full lockup when Redza needs to be identified from a distance.
- Use lettering alone for compact headers, social avatars and footer applications.
- Use the truck mark as a supporting symbol, not as a replacement for the wordmark on first contact.
- Use the white/red logo on Ink or Soft black.
- Use the black/red logo on White or Cream.
- Keep a clear space at least equal to the height of the red accent mark around the logo.
- Never stretch, rotate, recolor, outline or add effects to the logo.

## Social graphic rules

- Feed master: 1080 x 1350 px.
- Story master: 1080 x 1920 px.
- Keep headline text inside a 100 px safe margin.
- Keep critical CTA text inside a 180 px bottom safe area for platform controls.
- Use one headline, one proof line and one CTA.
